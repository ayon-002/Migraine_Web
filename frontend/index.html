About the Migraine Risk Index

The Migraine Risk Index (MRI) is a demonstration score, built for this project, that combines four monitored environmental factors into a single 0–100 reading:

  • Noise — sound level in decibels (dB)
  • CO₂ — carbon dioxide concentration in parts per million (ppm)
  • Light — ambient brightness in lux
  • Temperature — ambient temperature in °C

Each sensor reading is converted into a 0–100 trigger score, and the overall MRI reflects how these combined readings compare against the ranges below.

RangeRisk level
0–29Low
30–59Medium
60–79High
80–100Severe

This is an environmental risk indication for a university IoT project, not a medical diagnosis or clinical tool. Always consult a healthcare professional about migraine symptoms or triggers.
